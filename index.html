THE MODERN JIVE SKILLS TOOLKIT

Beta

A toolkit of 81 skills to draw on for strong social and competition dancing. Some skills are more or less critical at different levels of experience. Not every skill needs to be used in every dance.

Why this framework exists

  1. Help Dancers focus their practice on specific, nameable skills, instead of vague "just get better" improvement.
  2. Help Teachers diagnose what a student needs next, and plan group or private lessons around specific, nameable skills.
  3. Help Judges describe what they're seeing with more precise, specific language.

No single toolkit will capture Modern Jive perfectly, and not everyone will agree with every choice made here. This resource is built on a wide variety of sources to reflect a range of perspectives: analysis of publicly available Ceroc and Modern Jive information, including competition rules from across the UK, Australia and New Zealand, over 15 years of experience teaching group classes, private lessons, feedback sessions, and competition judging, and consultation with highly respected and experienced members of the Ceroc and Modern Jive communities across the UK, Australia and New Zealand.
